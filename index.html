I'm a researcher and engineer who transitioned from full-stack development to the scientific frontier of AI, Machine Learning & Robotics. After graduating with First Class Honors in Computer Science, I relocated to Bremen, Germany to pursue an MSc at the University of Bremen.

My current research focuses on Advanced Machine Learning, Perception (6D Pose Estimation), and Cognitive Robotics through the ACRAMbly master project. I believe the most important breakthroughs live at the intersection of software, hardware, and intelligence.

Previously I delivered 15+ production projects across Europe and the Middle East as a freelance developer, and held engineering roles at CandidateSide and Prime Missions (UK).
